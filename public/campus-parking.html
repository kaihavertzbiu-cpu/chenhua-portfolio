    THE RECOMMENDATION

    Your limits come first.

    The assistant first checks each lot against your arrival time, parking duration, budget, walking limit and any published restrictions included in this dataset. Ineligible lots do not receive a ranking score.

    01

    Check eligibility

    Filter parking options before comparing their scores.

    02

    Estimate availability

    Combine 60% simulated observations with 40% simulated historical patterns. Apply a demand adjustment for the event scenario. Outdated observations use history alone.

    03

    Rank your options

    Availability, estimated walking time and hourly cost receive a weighted score. Your sliders control the relative importance of each factor. A score of 80 is a ranking score, not an 80% chance of parking.

    04

    Choose a lot

    Select an eligible result to see the lot and destination, estimated walking time and a link to external walking directions.

    DATA & COVERAGE

    Know what the estimate means.

    Walking distance uses straight-line distance × 1.3 as a planning approximation, at 80 metres per minute. Rain increases the estimate according to simulated exposure. The map connection is not a surveyed walking path.

    This demonstration covers selected Cornell Ithaca short-term parking locations. It does not reserve spaces, take payment, check license plates or send availability notifications.
